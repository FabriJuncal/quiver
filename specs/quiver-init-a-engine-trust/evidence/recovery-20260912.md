twice after mistaking an empty initial observation for process completion. The
first execution's artifact was later overwritten by the second. Before that
overwrite the coordinator observed its header and TAP aggregate: 43/43, exit 0,

log and all full-CI output are retained above. No failed result or source change
was involved. This is one recovery verification cycle with a duplicate command,
not another implementation/fix cycle.

timeout or empty output is not a terminal state. Each subsequent attempt must
have a unique evidence path. Later reviewer handoff explicitly includes this rule.
